Effective 14.11.2026

BrightestMe helps you look after the parts of yourself that matter to you. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and what you can do about it. We collect as little as we can.

## 2. What we process and why

| Data | What it is | Why | Legal basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Account data | Your email address and an account ID. With Google sign-in, also the name Google shares with us. | To create your account, sign you in and send sign-in emails | Contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR |
| Your core | The parts you choose, their names and colours, your vessel design and settings | To show your core and sync it across devices | Contract, Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Tending history | Which part you tended on which day, and the glow levels calculated from it | To work out each part's glow and show your weekly reflection | Contract, Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Reminder settings | Whether reminders are on and the time you picked | To schedule reminders on your phone | Contract, Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Crash reports | Error details, app version, device model and Android version, with no names of your parts | To find and fix bugs | Legitimate interest in a working, secure app, Art. 6(1)(f) |
| Usage analytics (optional) | Which screens and features you use, with a random ID that is not linked to your account, email or location | To learn what helps and improve the app | Your consent, Art. 6(1)(a); off unless you turn it on |
| Server logs | IP address and time of requests to our backend | To keep the service secure and prevent abuse | Legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) |

You name your parts yourself. Please don't put sensitive details, like health information, in those names: we only use them to show your core back to you.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your data for profiling or automated decisions.

## 3. Where your data is stored

The app keeps your data on your phone first, so it works offline. When you're signed in and online, it syncs a copy to our backend so you can use it on another device and don't lose it.

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No reminder content passes through a server.

## 5. How long we keep data

- Your account, core and history: until you delete your account. Then we delete them from our database right away and from our encrypted backups within 30 days.
- Crash reports: up to 90 days.
- Analytics: up to 12 months, and deleted when you withdraw consent and ask us to.
- Server logs: up to 1 day.

Data on your phone stays until you delete the account, sign out, or uninstall the app.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to:

- see the data we hold about you (Art. 15)
- have it corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17)
- restrict its processing (Art. 18)
- receive it in a portable format (Art. 20)
- object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21)
- withdraw consent at any time, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). Turn analytics off with the "Anonymous usage data" switch in You → About.

You can delete your account yourself in the app (You → Account → Delete account and data), or on our [delete account page](/brightestme/delete-account). For anything else, email support@5minutesme.com.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. Ours is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

## 7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to your data on our backend is limited to your own account by database rules.

## 8. Children

BrightestMe is not meant for children under 16. We don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

## 9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. We'll tell you in the app before important changes take effect.
