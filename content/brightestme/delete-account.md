# Delete your BrightestMe account

BrightestMe is made by 5MinutesMe (Jasim Labadakath Zainudheen). You can delete your account and all its data at any time.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open BrightestMe and sign in.
2. Go to You → Account → Delete account and data.
3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you're signed out on this phone.

## Without the app

Email support@5minutesme.com from the address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my BrightestMe account". We'll delete it within 7 days and confirm by email. If you write from another address, we'll ask you to confirm it's your account first.

## What we delete

- Your account: email address, name from Google, account ID
- Your cores: parts, names, colours, vessel and settings
- Your tending history and glow levels
- Your reminder settings and analytics data linked to your account

## What we keep, and for how long

- Backups: copies in our encrypted database backups are removed within 30 days.
- Crash reports: they don't contain your parts and are deleted automatically within 90 days.
- Nothing else. We keep no copy of your core once it is deleted.

Data stored on your phone is deleted when you delete the account in the app. If you delete by email, uninstall the app to remove the copy on your phone.

Deleting is permanent and can't be undone. You can make a new account later, but it will start empty.
